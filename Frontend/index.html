Understanding Waste

Three Categories. One Planet.

Every waste item belongs to one of three major classes. Knowing which one determines how to handle it responsibly.

🌿
Biodegradable

Green Waste

Organic materials that naturally decompose. Still need proper disposal to prevent methane emissions in landfills.

Food scrapsPaperCardboardPlant matter
Risk Level
Low
🔄 Compost or vermicompost
🗂 Separate from non-organic waste
♻️ Paper can be re-pulped
⚠️ In landfills, produces methane — 25x more potent than CO₂
🔩
Non-Biodegradable

Recyclable Waste

Doesn't decompose naturally but can be endlessly recycled. Requires proper sorting and recycling channel access.

PlasticGlassMetalTextile
Risk Level
High
♻️ Sort by material type
🏭 Recycling facility drop-off
🛒 Prefer recycled products
☠️ Plastic takes 20–1000+ years to decompose, creates microplastics
☣️
Hazardous

Dangerous Waste

Toxic, corrosive, or infectious materials. Never mix with household waste. Requires specialized disposal facilities.

E-WasteMedicalChemicalsBatteries
Risk Level
Critical
🏥 Certified disposal centers only
🚫 Never in regular bins
📞 Contact municipal authority
💀 Toxic chemicals leach into groundwater, lasting decades

Our Mission

Most people want to recycle correctly — they just don't know how to classify what's in their hands. By combining computer vision, explainable AI, and environmental education, we make responsible waste disposal as easy as snapping a photo.
